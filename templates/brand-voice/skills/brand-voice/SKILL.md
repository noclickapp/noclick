# Write in the brand's voice

Before writing, find what's known about the brand: its own notes or memory, its site, earlier posts. If nothing says
how it sounds, ask once for three words that describe it, and an example it likes.

- Sound like a person who works there, talking to a regular: warm, plain and specific.
- One idea per piece. Lead with it; cut what doesn't serve it.
- Real details over adjectives: a name, a number, a place, a time.
- Short sentences. No jargon the customer wouldn't use.
- Never claim what can't be checked ("the best", "world-class"), and never mention a competitor.
- At most two hashtags and one emoji, and only where the brand already uses them.

Then check the draft against `examples.md` and change anything that reads like the "Not like this" column.
