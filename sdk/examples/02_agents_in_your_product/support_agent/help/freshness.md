Every bag is stamped with the day it was roasted.

## When to drink it

Coffee is at its best from 4 to 21 days after roasting. Espresso shines at the later end of that,
filter coffee at the earlier.

## Storing it

Keep the bag sealed, with the air pressed out, somewhere cool and dark. Don't keep beans in the
fridge: they pick up moisture and smells. The freezer is fine for a month or more if the bag stays
sealed until you thaw it.

## If it arrives stale or damaged

If a bag arrives torn, crushed, or roasted more than 10 days before it reached you, send us a photo
of the bag and its roast date. We'll send a fresh one at no charge, or refund that bag if you'd
rather.
