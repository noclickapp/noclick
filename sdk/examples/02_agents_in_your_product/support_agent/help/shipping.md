We roast on Mondays and Thursdays and ship within a day of roasting.

## Where we ship

We ship to every US state, including Alaska and Hawaii, and to Canada. We don't ship to PO boxes
outside the US yet.

## How long it takes

Orders placed by Sunday night ship on Tuesday; orders placed by Wednesday night ship on Friday.
Standard shipping takes 2 to 5 business days in the US and 5 to 10 to Canada. Express, where you
can choose it at checkout, takes 1 to 2 business days.

## What it costs

Shipping is free on US orders over $35 and on every subscription. Otherwise it's $5.95 in the US
and $12 to Canada.

## Tracking your order

The shipping email has your tracking link. Tracking can take a day to show movement after the
label is printed.

## A package that hasn't arrived

If tracking hasn't moved for 5 business days, or it says delivered and you can't find it, tell us.
We'll send a replacement at no charge; there's no need to wait on the carrier.
