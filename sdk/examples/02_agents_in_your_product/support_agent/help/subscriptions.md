A subscription sends you fresh coffee every 2, 3 or 4 weeks, with free shipping.

## Skip or pause a delivery

Skip the next delivery, or pause for up to three months, from Account > Subscription. Changes made
before 11pm Eastern the day before your roast day apply to the next delivery.

## Change your coffee or grind

Swap coffees, change the grind (whole bean, drip, espresso or French press) or the size from
Account > Subscription. Each delivery can be different.

## Cancel

Cancel at any time from Account > Subscription, with no fee. A delivery that has already been
roasted ships as usual, and you can ask for a refund on it like any other order.
