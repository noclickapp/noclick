We want every bag to be one you're glad you bought. If it isn't, we'll make it right.

## Who can get a refund

You can ask for a refund on any order within 30 days of its delivery. Opened bags count: coffee is
a matter of taste, and we'd rather know it didn't suit you. Subscription orders are refunded the
same way as one-off orders.

Gift cards and orders older than 30 days can't be refunded. If something went wrong with an older
order, write to us anyway and a person will look at it.

## How much you get back

A whole order is refunded in full, shipping included. For one bag out of several, we refund that
bag's price. If a bag arrived damaged or stale, see
[Damaged or stale coffee](https://help.northwindcoffee.test/freshness#if-it-arrives-stale-or-damaged):
we'd rather send a new one.

## How long refunds take

A refund goes back to the card you paid with. We issue it the same day, and it shows on your
statement within 5 to 10 business days, depending on your bank. You don't need to send the coffee
back.

## Large refunds

Refunds over $50 are checked by someone on our team before they're issued, usually within one
business day. You'll hear from us either way.
