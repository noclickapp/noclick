## planetscale_service_token (Planetscale)
name: Service token
line: Paste a service token from Planetscale.
how: Create a service token in your Planetscale organization settings and grant access for the databases and operations you use. Paste the token only, without Bearer. Use a current service token that supports Bearer authentication; legacy token-ID pairs are not supported. Connection verification lists your organizations. This is the management API, not a database connection password.
from: planetscale.com
  Service token
