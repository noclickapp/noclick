## 7shifts_access_token (7shifts)
name: Access token
line: Paste an access token from 7shifts.
how: In 7shifts, open Company Settings → Developer Tools → Access Tokens and create an access token. Paste it without the Bearer prefix. Access tokens grant admin access to one company and cannot be scoped. Leave Company GUID empty for an access token. A partner-issued bearer token may instead be pasted with its Company GUID; those tokens expire after one hour and must be replaced manually.
from: developers.7shifts.com
  Access token
  Company GUID (partner tokens only) (optional) — Leave empty for a personal access token. Partner bearer tokens require the company GUID from Connected Apps.
