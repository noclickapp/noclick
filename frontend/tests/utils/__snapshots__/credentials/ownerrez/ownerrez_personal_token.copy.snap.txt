## ownerrez_personal_token (Ownerrez)
name: Personal access token
line: Paste a personal access token from Ownerrez.
how: For your own Ownerrez account, create a Personal Access Token in Settings > Advanced Tools > Developer/API Settings and grant access to yourself. Enter your account email and pt_ token. Ownerrez limits personal tokens to two accounts per IP within 24 hours; partner deployments need OAuth. Messages require OAuth and are unavailable with this connection.
from: app.ownerrez.com
  Account email — Your Ownerrez sign-in email.
