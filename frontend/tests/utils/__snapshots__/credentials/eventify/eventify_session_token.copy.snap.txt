## eventify_session_token (Eventify)
name: Session token
line: Paste a Session token from Eventify.
how: Paste the session_token for your event from Eventify's generateToken API. NoClick lists your associated events to check the connection. Replace the token here if it expires; NoClick does not store your Eventify password or generate or refresh tokens.
from: adminapi.eventify.io
