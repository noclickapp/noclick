## akeneo_token (Akeneo)
name: Bearer token
line: Paste a bearer token from Akeneo.
how: Enter your Akeneo PIM HTTPS URL and an existing bearer access token. Connector tokens normally expire after one hour: replace an expired token here. An existing App token can also be pasted. NoClick does not generate or refresh tokens. Grant read access to products for connection verification and the permissions needed for your operations.
from: api.akeneo.com
  PIM URL — Your Akeneo PIM origin, for example https://my-pim.cloud.akeneo.com
  Access token
