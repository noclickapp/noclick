## clicksign_access_token (Clicksign)
name: Access token
line: Paste an access token from Clicksign.
how: Generate an access token in your Clicksign account settings and paste it here without a Bearer prefix. Choose the environment that issued the token. NoClick reads your envelopes to verify the connection.
from: developers.clicksign.com
  Access token
  Environment (optional) — Use the environment that issued your access token.
