## penpot_token (Penpot)
name: Personal access token
line: Paste a personal access token from Penpot.
how: In Penpot, open Your account > Integrations > Personal access tokens. Create a token, choose its expiration, and paste the token here. It gives access to your account until it expires or you delete it.
