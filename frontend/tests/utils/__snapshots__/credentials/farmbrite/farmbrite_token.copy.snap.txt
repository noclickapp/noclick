## farmbrite_token (Farmbrite)
name: Personal access token
line: Paste a personal access token from Farmbrite.
how: In Farmbrite, open your user profile and copy the API Key. Paste it here without the Bearer prefix. The token inherits your user's role and permissions. Farmbrite recommends a separate integration user. NoClick checks access by reading your tasks.
from: developers.farmbrite.com
