## imap_smtp (Imap)
name: App password
line: Paste an app password.
how: Sign in with your email address and an app password, a password your provider makes for one app: most no longer let apps use your normal password. Yahoo Mail: https://login.yahoo.com/account/security (Generate app password). AOL: https://login.aol.com/account/security (Generate app password). iCloud Mail: https://account.apple.com (Sign-In and Security, then App-Specific Passwords). Zoho Mail: https://accounts.zoho.com/home#security/app_password (and turn on Imap access in Zoho Mail's settings). Fastmail: https://app.fastmail.com/settings/security (Manage app passwords). GMX and mail.com: turn on POP3 & Imap access in the mailbox's settings (mail.com has it with Premium). For these, NoClick knows the servers. For a company or any other mailbox, open More options and enter the Imap and SMTP servers your provider gives. Gmail and Outlook.com don't take an app password here: use NoClick's Gmail or Outlook step, which signs in with Google or Microsoft.
  IMAP server (optional) — Filled in for Yahoo, AOL, iCloud, Zoho, Fastmail, GMX and mail.com addresses.
  IMAP port (optional)
  IMAP security (optional)
  SMTP server (optional) — Filled in for Yahoo, AOL, iCloud, Zoho, Fastmail, GMX and mail.com addresses.
  SMTP port (optional)
  SMTP security (optional)
  Email address
  App password — The app password your provider made for NoClick, not your normal password.
  Username (optional) — Only if your server signs in with something other than your email address.
