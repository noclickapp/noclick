## motaword_access_token (Motaword)
name: Access token
line: Paste an access token from Motaword.
how: In your Motaword developer account, choose your application in the Authorization Tool and copy its access token. Request privileged scope for private endpoints. Paste only the token. Temporary tokens must be replaced when they expire. NoClick checks your account with GET /me.
