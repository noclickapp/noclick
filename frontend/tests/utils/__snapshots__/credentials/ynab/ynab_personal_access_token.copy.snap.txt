## ynab_personal_access_token (Ynab)
name: Personal access token
line: Paste a personal access token from Ynab.
how: In Ynab Account Settings, open Developer Settings and generate a Personal Access Token. Paste the token for your own account. NoClick uses it to read and update your plans. Amounts are in milliunits: 1000 is one currency unit.
from: app.ynab.com
