## savvycal_token (Savvycal)
name: Private key
line: Paste a private key from Savvycal.
how: In Savvycal, open Settings > Developers, create a personal access token, and paste its Private Key. NoClick uses it to read your account and manage events, scheduling links and webhooks. Use the private key, not the public key.
