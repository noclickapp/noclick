## clevertap_passcode (Clevertap)
name: Account Passcode
line: Paste an account Passcode from Clevertap.
how: In Clevertap Settings, copy your Account ID and generate an account passcode under Passcodes. Choose the region holding your account. NoClick checks the account's subscription groups before saving.
from: developer.clevertap.com
  Account ID
  Account passcode
  Account region (optional) — India: in1; Singapore: sg1; United States: us1; Indonesia: aps3; UAE: mec1; Europe: eu1.
