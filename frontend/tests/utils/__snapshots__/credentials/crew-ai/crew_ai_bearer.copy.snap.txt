## crew_ai_bearer (Crew AI)
name: Bearer token
line: Paste a bearer token from Crew AI.
how: Open your deployed crew in CrewAI AMP and copy its deployment URL and Bearer Token or User Bearer Token from the Status tab. Paste the token without the Bearer prefix. NoClick reads this deployment's inputs to check access; this does not verify your identity or write permissions.
from: docs.crewai.com
  Deployment URL — Your crew's HTTPS origin, such as https://my-travel-crew.crewai.com.
  Bearer token
