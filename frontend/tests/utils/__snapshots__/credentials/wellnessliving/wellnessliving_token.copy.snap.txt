## wellnessliving_token (Wellnessliving)
name: Bearer token
line: Paste a bearer token from Wellnessliving.
how: Paste an existing Wellnessliving JWT bearer token, without the Bearer prefix, and your business ID. Use a staff token with permission for your operations. The production API documents token issuance at GET /Core/Passport/Enter/Jwt/JwtToken.json after signing in through your own approved API application. NoClick does not sign in or renew this token; replace it when it expires. Choose the data centre that serves your business.
