## youtrack_token (Youtrack)
name: Permanent token
line: Paste a Permanent token from Youtrack.
how: Enter your Youtrack installation URL and a permanent token from your profile's Account Security tab. Select the Youtrack service scope; your account permissions control which issues you can read and change.
