## Why NoClick asks for this

NoClick Community is licensed under the AGPL-3.0-only. NoClick also runs the
same code as a hosted service with proprietary additions, and offers commercial
licenses to companies that cannot accept the AGPL. A contribution offered under
the AGPL alone would bind NoClick to the AGPL's terms for those uses. This
agreement is what lets NoClick keep accepting outside contributions while
keeping both the open project and the company behind it viable.

## How to sign

Post this sentence, exactly, as a comment on your pull request:

> I have read the CLA Document and I hereby sign the CLA

The CLA bot records your GitHub username and the time on the `cla-signatures`
branch of this repository, and does not ask again. Comment `recheck` if the
status check has not updated.
